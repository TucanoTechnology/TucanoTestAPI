# Ticket Management Policy

## Ticket creation

- Every change must have a ticket before implementation begins.
- Tickets should clearly describe the goal, scope, and expected outcome.
- Link related tickets, PRs, and requirements where applicable.
- Use the ticket template for new work whenever possible.

## Ticket hygiene

- Keep ticket titles concise and descriptive.
- Maintain a single source of truth in the ticket description.
- Record important decisions, blockers, and scope changes in comments.
- Update labels and priority when the work changes.
- Avoid leaving stale or ambiguous tickets open without context.

## Ticket closure

- Confirm that the work matches the definition of done.
- Add a final summary comment before closing.
- Reference the PR or merged changes in the ticket.
- Close the ticket only after the implementation is verified.
